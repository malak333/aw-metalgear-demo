METAL GEAR SOLID

Unofficial Fan Overview — PlayStation, 1998

Introduction Mission Characters Stealth Legacy

Introduction

Metal Gear Solid is a stealth-action game developed by Konami Computer Entertainment Japan and released for the original PlayStation in 1998. Directed by Hideo Kojima, the game combined cinematic presentation with tactical espionage gameplay, establishing a new benchmark for interactive storytelling and earning its place as one of the most influential video games of its era.

Mission

The rogue unit FOXHOUND occupies the Shadow Moses facility in Alaska, threatening a nuclear strike. Solid Snake, an elite special-operations soldier, is deployed to infiltrate the facility and stop the threat.

Characters

Solid Snake

The game's protagonist, Solid Snake is a legendary soldier renowned for his expertise in infiltration and close-quarters combat. Deployed into Shadow Moses, Snake must rely on his tactical instincts and stealth skills to navigate hostile territory and complete his mission.

Liquid Snake

Liquid Snake commands the FOXHOUND forces at Shadow Moses. A formidable adversary with a complicated history tied to Snake, he leads FOXHOUND and drives much of the conflict facing Snake throughout the game.

Otacon

Otacon is an engineer encountered at Shadow Moses supporting Snake. He provides technical support over the Codec.

Stealth & Gameplay

Metal Gear Solid centered its design around three interlocking systems. Stealth is paramount: Snake must avoid detection by studying guard patrol routes, using cover and environmental obstacles, and deciding when to sneak, distract, or confront enemies.

A radar display shows enemy positions and fields of vision. When enemies spot Snake, the alert level rises and they converge on his last known position; the player must evade to break the search.

The Codec — an in-universe encrypted communication system — provides communication and advice. Through it, Snake receives mission updates and consults allies for guidance.

Legacy

Metal Gear Solid (1998) reshaped the expectations of what an action game could achieve. Its blend of cinematic storytelling, complex characters, and genuine tactical depth influenced countless developers and helped define the stealth genre. Decades later, the original PlayStation release remains a landmark title, celebrated for its ambition, atmosphere, and lasting cultural impact.
